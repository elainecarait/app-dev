# My Favorite Series: The Summer I Turned Pretty

## About the Series

**The Summer I Turned Pretty** is a coming-of-age romantic drama series based on the book trilogy by Jenny Han.

The story follows *Isabel "Belly" Conklin* and her summer experiences at Cousins Beach, where she faces friendship, love, family, and growing up.

## Why I Love This Series

I like this series because it shows how relationships can change as people grow older. It also has a mix of romance, friendship, family moments, and emotional scenes that make the story interesting.

> "Everything good, everything magical happens between the months of June and August."

## Main Characters

- **Belly Conklin** – The main character of the story.
- **Conrad Fisher** – One of the Fisher brothers and an important part of Belly's story.
- **Jeremiah Fisher** – Conrad's brother, who also has a special relationship with Belly.
- **Steven Conklin** – Belly's older brother.
- **Taylor Jewel** – Belly's best friend.

## What Makes It Interesting?

1. The summer setting at Cousins Beach.
2. The complicated relationships between the characters.
3. The friendship and family moments.
4. The emotional story and unexpected situations.
